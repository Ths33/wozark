Manual analysis predicted 52-53°F for KSEA, actual was 51-52°F (1 bucket off). Jonah predicted 48-49°F (2 buckets off).

**Why the miss:** Solar radiation was 604 W/m² (strong sun) which suggested more warming, but wind was forecast at 22-27mph and likely materialized. Strong wind counteracts solar heating — the combination capped the peak at 51°F.

**How to apply:** When enriching Jonah's analysis or doing manual predictions:
- Solar radiation alone is not enough — must be weighted against wind speed
- High solar + low wind = temperature rises above forecast
- High solar + high wind = temperature caps near forecast or below
- This interaction (solar vs wind) should be a key pattern for Jonah to learn
